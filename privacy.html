Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Subhashini Signature Services Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit the Vantara at S-Cube website (the "Site") or submit an enquiry to us. By using the Site or contacting us, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you fill in an enquiry or callback form on the Site, we collect the details you provide, which include your full name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, and the country you are currently based in. We also collect any information you choose to share when you call, email, or message us.

Information collected automatically. When you browse the Site, we and our partners use cookies and similar technologies (see Section 4) to collect technical and usage data such as your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring website, and actions you take — including form submissions.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and share project details such as pricing, floor plans, and rental-income information.
  • To contact you by phone, WhatsApp, or email about Vantara at S-Cube.
  • For marketing and remarketing, including showing you relevant advertisements on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram).
  • To measure advertising performance and improve our Site and services.
  • To comply with our legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Legal Basis

We process your information on the basis of your consent (which you give by submitting a form or contacting us), our legitimate business interest in responding to and following up on enquiries, and where necessary to comply with the law.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use the Meta Pixel, a tool provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. It uses cookies to record page views and events (such as form submissions) and helps us measure and optimise our advertising on Facebook and Instagram. You can control cookies through your browser settings and manage your advertising preferences within your Facebook and Instagram account settings.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — for advertising and measurement, via the Meta Pixel and, where applicable, the Conversions API.
  • Service providers who help us operate our business, such as website hosting, customer-relationship-management (CRM), and communication tools.
  • Authorities or third parties where required by law, regulation, or legal process, or to protect our rights.

6. Third-Party Services

The Site uses the Meta Pixel, an embedded Google Maps location map, and Google Fonts. These services are provided by Meta and Google and may collect data (such as your IP address) in accordance with their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

7. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. Please note that some information you enter may be temporarily stored in your browser and transmitted to us when you submit a form. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and for legitimate business and legal purposes, after which we delete or anonymise it.

9. International Data Transfers

We are based in India. If you access the Site or contact us from outside India — for example, as a non-resident Indian (NRI) — your information will be transferred to and processed in India.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent or opt out of marketing at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. You may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time.

11. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
